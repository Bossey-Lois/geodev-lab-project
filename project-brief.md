# My project brief

## The question
Which settlements in Akinyele Local Government Area, Oyo State, have no paved road within 2 km?

## Why It Matters
Access to paved roads affects transportation, access to markets, healthcare, schools, and overall economic development. Identifying settlements that are more than 2 km from a paved road can help government agencies and planners prioritize road infrastructure investments and improve accessibility for rural communities. The results could also support transport planning and rural development initiatives within Akinyele LGA.

## The data I need 
- Settlement locations- GRID3- https://data.grid3.org -GeoPackage -1.9GB
- Settlement names- GRID3- https://data.grid3.org -GeoPackage -192kb
- Road network data- OSM via QuickOSM -extracted for the LGA
- Road classification data- OSM-extracted for the LGA
- Administrative boundary- GRID3- https://data.grid3.org -GeoPackage -4.2MB

## What I Would Build
I would create an accessibility map based on GIS that displays all settlements in Akinyele LGA along with their distance to the closest paved road. Settlements situated over 2 km from a paved road would be marked as underserved areas. The end result could feature a map, a ranked list of the impacted settlements, and a dashboard that planners can utilize to pinpoint priority areas for upcoming road development.
